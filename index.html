What is the Flamehaven Experiment Archive?

The Flamehaven Experiment Archive is a transparent, decentralized public ledger of all capability evaluations, mathematical physics verification runs, and sovereign biological intelligence safety audits executed by Flamehaven Labs.

Every log and audit report published here is fully local, deterministic, and verifiable—established strictly without runtime hazards, network dependencies, or external LLM layers to guarantee absolute auditing integrity and reproducibility.
