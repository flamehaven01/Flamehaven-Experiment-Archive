## 4. Technical Conclusion: Beyond Textual Semantics
The repeated failure to significantly improve SR9 beyond ~0.30, even with highly distilled and externally validated linguistic inputs, indicates a fundamental limitation of the **BioLinkBERT-base encoder's textual semantic space** for complex biomedical hypotheses.
- **Hypothesis:** The encoder struggles to map high-dimensional structural and genomic data (AlphaFold, AlphaGenome) directly into a stable linguistic representation that it deems "high confidence."
- **Next Frontier:** Achieving higher semantic stability (SR9 > 0.80) will require moving beyond purely linguistic inputs and exploring **multimodal fusion**, where structural coordinates and genomic embeddings are directly integrated into the NNSL's latent space.
