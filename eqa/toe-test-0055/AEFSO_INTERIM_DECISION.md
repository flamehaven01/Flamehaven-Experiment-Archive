# AEFSO Interim Decision

**Target**: `AEFSO`  
**Date**: `2026-04-18`  
**Decision mode**: `post-Run-3 interim classification`

---

## Executive Decision

**Current Flamehaven-TOE decision**:

- `DO NOT PROMOTE TO CORE`
- `KEEP ALIVE AS OPTIONAL BACKEND REPRESENTATION LAYER`
- `CONTINUE ONLY FOR MISSING-LINK / IR DISCOVERY VALUE`

This is the most defensible decision after:

- SPAR paper review
- fhval-aligned validation
- dogfood Runs 1–4

---

## What Has Been Proven So Far

AEFSO has survived three distinct gates.

### Gate 1 — Paper admissibility

It is a serious mathematical representation claim, not empty hype.

### Gate 2 — Validation discipline

It can be discussed under explicit assumptions and bounded claims without
silently upgrading itself into a TOE core proposition.

### Gate 3 — First TOE contact

It survives primitive and early helper-level contact strongly enough to remain
interesting.

That is already meaningful.

### Gate 4 — Governance-surface contact

It does not justify promotion onto governance-facing score and inequality
surfaces.

---

## What Has Not Been Proven

The experiment has **not** shown that AEFSO should become:

- canonical TOE mathematical form
- default evaluator representation
- clearer human-facing expression layer
- better geometry-facing contract language

That gap is no longer hypothetical. It is visible in the dogfood pattern.

---

## Evidence Pattern Across Runs

### Run 1

AEFSO is strongest on:

- `exp(x)`
- `ln(x)`
- primitive `eml(x, y)` identities

Interpretation:

- strong primitive basis
- promising normalization/search surface

### Run 2

AEFSO weakens on:

- multiplication-heavy helper expressions
- `linear_dilaton` analytical surfaces

Interpretation:

- representability survives
- clarity and cost begin to push back

### Run 3

AEFSO is weakest where TOE already has:

- compact
- geometric
- reviewable
- analytically transparent
  anchors

Interpretation:

- the cleaner the native TOE contract, the less AEFSO helps as a front-facing
  representation

---

## Interim Classification

### Rejected for now

- `CORE_CANDIDATE`

Reason:

- the experiment has reached a limit for core promotion before even entering the
  hardest physics surfaces

### Approved for now

- `OPTIONAL_REPRESENTATION_LAYER`

Meaning:

- allowed for symbolic normalization experiments
- allowed for compiler/search/IR investigation
- not approved for replacement of validated TOE analytical forms
- not approved for governance-facing mathematical surfaces

### Increased probability

- `MISSING_LINK_DISCOVERED`

Meaning:

- AEFSO may be most valuable as the pressure test that reveals a better
  intermediate representation

---

## Operational Recommendation

Inside Flamehaven-TOE, treat AEFSO as:

- research-only
- optional
- backend-facing

Do **not** treat AEFSO as:

- human-facing canonical math
- default SPAR anchor language
- evaluator-default notation

---

## Why The Experiment Should Continue

The experiment should continue **not** because AEFSO still looks like the TOE
core, but because it is now doing something more interesting:

- exposing exactly where TOE prefers clean native expressions
- exposing where uniform normalization helps
- exposing the likely shape of a better intermediate IR

That is enough to justify deeper bounded testing.

---

## Approval Threshold For Any Future Upgrade

AEFSO should only be reconsidered for stronger promotion if later runs show all
of the following:

1. bounded complexity on beta residual surfaces
2. no meaningful loss of SPAR reviewability
3. no degradation of anchor clarity on Omega / EFT surfaces
4. a concrete advantage over current native expression forms

Without those, stronger promotion is not justified.

---

## One-Line Decision

AEFSO is now approved to continue only as an optional backend research
normalization/search layer, and not approved for Flamehaven-TOE core or
governance-facing mathematical promotion.
